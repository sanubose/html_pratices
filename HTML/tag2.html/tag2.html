ROSCOSMOS,

ROSCOSMOS, the Russian space agency, is one of the oldest and most experienced space organizations in the world. It was responsible for historic achievements such as launching the first artificial satellite (Sputnik) and sending the first human, Yuri Gagarin, into space. Today, ROSCOSMOS continues to play a major role in human spaceflight and international missions, especially through its collaboration on the International Space Station (ISS).


NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration)

NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration) is the United States' space agency and one of the most advanced in the world. Established in 1958, NASA has led groundbreaking missions such as the Apollo Moon landings, Mars rover explorations, and deep-space telescopes like the James Webb Space Telescope. It continues to push the boundaries of space science, human exploration, and innovation.


ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)

ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation) is India’s national space agency, known for its cost-effective and innovative missions. ISRO gained global recognition with missions like Chandrayaan (Moon mission) and Mangalyaan (Mars Orbiter Mission). It is also working on human spaceflight through the Gaganyaan program and continues to expand India’s presence in space exploration.


JAXA (Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency)

JAXA (Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency) is Japan’s space agency, known for its advanced research and precision missions. It has successfully conducted asteroid sample-return missions like Hayabusa and contributes to the International Space Station with its Kibo laboratory module. JAXA focuses on scientific research, Earth observation, and innovative space technologies.
